The idea: while the bar is untucked (your manual reveal / snooze), the menu-bar glyph becomes this filled-in T instead of the generic timer — the icon shows its own state. The dashed→solid fill also makes a clean loop for the website hero and the in-app reveal. The small column shows it at glyph size: the solid end-state reads fine; the dashes only resolve when large, so the menu bar would use the solid form. Timing, fill direction (down-from-bar vs. rise-up), and easing are all tunable.
